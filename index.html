ABOUT THE EXPERIENCE

A living study
in motion.

FLORA pairs a browser-side MediaPipe hand-landmark model with a procedural Three.js botanical scene. Your camera feed is analyzed locally after you grant permission; frames are not uploaded or stored by this app.

The flowers are articulated 3D meshes so every petal can move in a controlled way. Real 3D Gaussian Splatting is reserved for optional scanned environment assets; no splat asset is currently loaded, and the flower meshes are not mislabeled as splats.

ML MODELMediaPipe Hand Landmarker · 21 keypoints
RENDERERThree.js · procedural petal geometry
SHORTCUTS← → flower · Space bloom · R reset · 1–4 scenes
